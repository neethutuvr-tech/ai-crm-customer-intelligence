# Campaign and next-best-action playbook

## Offer selection

Use customer interests, order history, current engagement and campaign rules to draft a relevant offer hypothesis. Preserve margin and avoid repeating a promotion the customer has recently received. A purchase propensity score is a ranking signal; it does not prove that a campaign caused a purchase.

## Contact policy

Before creating a task or sending a campaign, confirm the contact channel is permitted, the customer has not opted out, the frequency cap is respected and the offer terms are valid. Suppress customers with an unresolved service issue from promotional automation until the issue has been reviewed.

## Measurement

Define the outcome and observation window before launch. Compare treatment and holdout groups, report uncertainty, and measure incremental outcomes rather than raw conversion alone. Record campaign version, audience definition and decision date so a result can be reproduced.
