# Retention review playbook

## Risk signal

A churn probability estimates the chance of a defined future outcome for a population similar to the training sample. It is not a certainty about one person. Review its time horizon, class balance, calibration and false-positive cost before using a threshold.

## Suggested human workflow

1. Check the customer's latest purchase and open support cases.
2. Compare churn probability with the team's capacity and a validated operating threshold.
3. Review contact permissions, recent campaign frequency and opt-out status.
4. Select a relevant service or retention response and record the reason in the CRM.
5. Measure outcomes with a holdout group where appropriate.

## Guardrails

Do not infer personal circumstances from location, purchase history or model scores. Do not promise that an offer will prevent churn. A model score can prioritize review, but a human owns the customer contact decision. Monitor precision and recall by time period and investigate drift.
